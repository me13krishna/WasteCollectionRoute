Plans the current points with 1, 2, 3 … trucks and stops one truck after the shortest total distance. Too few trucks mean long routes, unload trips and overtime; too many each add their own trip out to the area. The bottom of that U is the optimal fleet. Distances here are illustrative demo values; the routes drawn use your real points and roads.
